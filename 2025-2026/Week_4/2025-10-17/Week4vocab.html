Week 4 Vocabulary

< img >

Embeds an image to a document or link.

< br >

Breaks off sentences acting like a separator.

< div >

Makes a box that nothing can be typed in, but could also be used as a separator.

< a >

Creates hyperlinks that could be replaced as a word.

Highest level section and makes text between it big as in example
